See the platform

You have agents.Now make them trustworthy.

Foundation AI sits behind every agent and question as a single governed intelligence source your enterprise owns.

  • Business users & analysts

    Ask your enterprise

    Get sourced, permission-aware answers across every system, whether the question comes from Copilot, Dashboards.AI or a custom app. Turn any answer into a live tile.

    “How many Abu Dhabi arrivals are delayed today, and why?”

  • Operations experts

    Capture know-how

    Keep the expertise nobody wrote down, along with the context and decisions behind it. Corrected procedures become reusable skills for the whole organization.

    “How do we price ground handling for a new carrier at AUH?”

  • Every employee

    Agents that learn from you

    Each user's agent starts empty and learns from every interaction. It spots repeated questions and offers to automate them, within that user's permissions.

    “You ask about cargo tonnage every Monday. Want this sent to you each week?”

  • Duty & ops managers

    Run the operation

    Spot crew shortfalls and SLA risk early, weigh the trade-offs, and act. Every human override teaches the system what good looks like.

    “Which freighter should we prioritize if we're three crew short?”

  • COO & executive team

    Plan with what-ifs

    Go beyond reporting to scenario planning, showing sources and confidence levels. See what pulling one lever does to the rest of the business.

    “If we add a shift at peak, what happens to turnaround time?”

  • AI & agents

    Connect AI to your intelligence

    Give Copilot, Claude or ChatGPT one well-informed tool in place of dozens of confusing ones. Foundation AI handles routing, context and permissions underneath, so answers are faster and more consistent.

    “What does this data mean, where does it live, and who is allowed to see it?”
